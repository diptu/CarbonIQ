# CarbonIQ Non-Technical Guide

## 🌏 What is CarbonIQ?
CarbonIQ is a cloud-based platform that helps Australian businesses measure, track, and report their **Scope 2 carbon emissions**. It is designed to simplify the reporting process, ensure compliance with **Climate Active** and **ASRS** standards, and provide actionable insights.

## 💡 Why Use CarbonIQ?
- **Save Time:** Automates the tedious work of processing energy bills and meter data.
- **Accurate Reporting:** Provides precise emissions calculations, ensuring regulatory compliance.
- **Audit-Ready:** Reports are formatted to meet Climate Active standards.
- **AI Insights:** Detects unusual energy consumption patterns and explains anomalies.
- **Secure Multi-Tenant Access:** Each organization’s data is kept private and secure.

## 🌱 Key Features
1. **Data Upload** – Easily upload your energy bills in CSV or PDF format.
2. **Automatic Emissions Calculation** – Calculates emissions based on energy usage and local grid intensity.
3. **Renewables Matching** – Tracks your GreenPower, REC, or PPA contributions and shows how they offset your emissions.
4. **Report Generation** – Generate downloadable PDF and CSV reports for internal use or regulatory submissions.
5. **AI Estimation** – If some data is missing, the AI fills gaps to provide accurate load profiles.
6. **Onboarding Wizard** – Step-by-step guidance for new users to set up their organization.
7. **Notifications** – Get email or in-app alerts when reports are ready or anomalies are detected.

## 📈 How CarbonIQ Works
1. **Sign Up & Onboard** – Create your account, set up your organization, and provide basic info.
2. **Upload Data** – Upload your bills and meter readings.
3. **Automated Processing** – The platform reads your data, standardizes it, and calculates emissions.
4. **Apply Renewable Offsets** – Link your GreenPower or REC purchases to reduce reported emissions.
5. **Generate Reports** – Download ready-to-submit reports for Climate Active or internal purposes.
6. **Insights & Notifications** – Receive AI-generated insights and alerts to make energy efficiency decisions.

## 🏢 Who Should Use CarbonIQ?
- Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Australia.
- Sustainability officers and energy managers.
- Advisors and consultants supporting clients with carbon reporting.
- Organizations aiming for transparent, audit-ready Scope 2 reporting.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy
- **Data Privacy:** Each organization’s data is completely isolated.
- **Secure Storage:** Sensitive data like bills and meter IDs are encrypted.
- **Access Control:** Only authorized users can access specific tenant data.
- **Compliance:** Built to support regulatory and reporting standards.

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Sign up for an account.
2. Complete the onboarding wizard for your organization.
3. Upload your energy bills (PDF or CSV).
4. Review calculated emissions and apply renewable offsets.
5. Generate your first Climate Active report.
6. Set up notifications to stay informed on updates and anomalies.
